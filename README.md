# One Piece - The Ultimate Treasure

## 🏴‍☠️ Overview

**One Piece** is the name given to all the treasure amassed by the Pirate King **Gol D. Roger**. The treasure is said to be of unimaginable value and is located on **Laugh Tale**, the final island of the Grand Line.

> **Legend**: "My treasure? If you want it, I'll let you have it. Look for it! I left all of it at that place!" - Gol D. Roger's final words

## 🎯 Character Overview

**Monkey D. Luffy** is the main protagonist of One Piece, captain of the Straw Hat Pirates, and currently one of the **Four Emperors** of the sea. His ultimate goal is to find the legendary treasure **One Piece** and become the **Pirate King**.
